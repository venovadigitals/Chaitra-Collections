Since the loom to your wardrobe

Woven with Heritage

Chaitra Collections was born from a simple belief: every saree carries a story older than the woman who wears it. We work directly with weaver clusters across Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Varanasi and Kanchipuram, bringing their craft to your doorstep without the markup of the middleman.

Every piece is checked by hand for weave, finish and colourfastness before it reaches you, so tradition arrives exactly as it was intended.
