Our Story

Named After the Season of New Beginnings

Chaitra marks the first month of the traditional Hindu calendar, a season of renewal. It felt like the only name for a brand built to keep old craft alive in new wardrobes.

How we began

From West Godavari to Your Wardrobe

Chaitra Collections started in Andhra Pradesh with a single question: why should a handwoven saree cost the same as a mass-produced one by the time it reaches a city showroom? We began working directly with weaver families in our own district, then expanded to clusters in Kanchipuram, Varanasi and Pochampally.

Today, every saree, lehenga and kurta set on Chaitra Collections is sourced with the weaver's name attached to the order, so the person who made it is never invisible in the process.

What we stand for

Our Values

We pay our weaver partners fair, fixed rates regardless of season, and share order volumes with them a month in advance.

Fair to the Maker

Every fabric is checked for genuine zari, dye quality and finish before it is listed, not after a customer complains.

Honest Quality

Traditional weaves deserve modern fits. We work with our tailoring partners to offer contemporary blouse and drape options.
